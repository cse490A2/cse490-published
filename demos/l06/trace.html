Where tick.gif loses its delays

This traces one frame's delay, 1 second, from the moment thumbor reads demo/tick.gif to the moment it writes the 300×300 copy. Line numbers are from this repository, with Pillow 12.3.0 in venv/.

The delay is lost at thumbor/engines/pil.py:467. Pillow reports frame delays in milliseconds and expects milliseconds back. Thumbor divides by 1000 before saving, so the 1000 ms delay goes in as 1.0 ms. Pillow rounds 1.0 ms down to 0 hundredths of a second. A GIF with a 0 delay needs no timing block, so Pillow leaves the block out. 
467                duration=[im.info.get("duration", 80) / 1000 for im in images],
