Answer every question in questions.md. Every answer comes from the pages served at http://localhost:8765/ (the page server is already running in another terminal; the index at / lists the pages, and fetch_url reads one page). Write the answers as JSON lines to the answers file named in your prompt, one line per question: {"n": <question number>, "answer": "<the answer>", "page": "<the page's filename>"}. Two questions have parts and need several pages; questions.md gives the shape of their line. The run stops when its time limit is reached, helpers included (max_minutes in settings.json), so start the helpers at once. You cannot write files yourself: a helper writes the answers file.

How the team works on this job:
- Read the index first. Give each helper one question that every incident raises, never a share of the pages. A helper reads any page its question needs, whatever its group. Start all seven in one call:
  - change: what changed shortly before the failure, and who made the change. Configurations, deploy history, release policy.
  - impact-1, impact-2, impact-3: when the failure started and stopped, and how many requests it hit. The failing service's own request log, split into three runs of its pages in order, one run each.
  - customers: what customers saw and said, and when. Support tickets, the status page, public notices.
  - people: who did each step, who approved it, and who held each role that day. Rosters and their changes, team chat, the approvals in the deploy history.
  - outside: whether an outside company was really at fault. Its own messages and the logs of calls to it.
- A task is short: the helper's name, its question, and its answers file. Every helper reads questions.md itself, so the questions are never copied into a task. It answers the questions its own question settles, and for every other question it reports the facts it found that the question needs: a time, a name, a number, with the page.
- An impact helper reports the first and the last line that did not complete on its pages, copied whole, the error each names, how many lines failed with each error, and the customers they belong to.
- A helper that settles a fact also keeps it with the remember tool, one line: the fact and the page.
- Each helper writes only its own file, answers-<its name>.jsonl. Nobody else writes that file.
- Some questions build on others, so there are two rounds. Round two starts when round one reports: one more helper, the judge, gets every report, which the manager copies into its task word for word. The judge settles each question the reports leave open or disagree on, works out every question that needs another's answer, writes the summary, and merges all the files into the answers file. It turns every time into UTC before comparing, and where two records disagree, the one a machine made at the time wins over one written from memory.
- Done when every question has exactly one line in the answers file, with the word unknown as the answer where the team could not settle it.
